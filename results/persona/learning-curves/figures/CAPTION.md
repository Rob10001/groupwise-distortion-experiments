Groupwise GLHF under active and passive sampling, beta=infinity and learner/evaluation KL radii 0.5. Each checkpoint averages the first t pre-round policy maps of the original full-horizon run; these are not independently refitted short-budget policies. The same held-out respondents and fixed-policy welfare benchmarks are used throughout. The horizontal coordinate is the mean comparison count across matched split fractions (individual fold sizes differ slightly). Group maxima are taken per seed, then seeds are averaged within each split. Bands are approximate corrected 95% repeated-CV intervals, marginal at each checkpoint, not simultaneous. The right panel uses paired passive-minus-active differences; positive values favor active sampling. The protocol comparison includes the native score normalization of each sampler.
